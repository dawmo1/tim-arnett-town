## Bettendorf / Le Claire

**Isle Casino Hotel Bettendorf** (1777 Isle Pkwy; 41.5232, -90.5075). HBG design, land casino opened 2016.
Complex ~195 x 156 m on the riverbank (river to the south): two hotel towers (12 storeys and ~8 storeys,
509 rooms), a single-storey 35,000 sq ft casino in the courtyard between them, a new grand entrance and joint
lobby on the land (north, Isle Pkwy / State St) side, a 115 x 75 m parking garage to the east (41.5229, -90.5060)
with a 2nd-level skywalk. Waterfront Convention Center (2021 State St, 41.5248, -90.5054): 98 x 69 m, 2009.
Materials [G]: tan precast #C9B79C, blue-green glass #3E6B78, red lit ISLE sign #C8102E.
Interior: ~1,000 slots, 19 tables, Lone Wolf bar on the floor, Keller's grill, Farmer's Pick buffet [finishes G:
patterned carpet, dark ceiling with light coves].

**TBK Bank Sports Complex** (4850 Competition Dr; 41.5926, -90.4644). Flat-roofed box ~179 x 172 m
(~270k sq ft), 12–15 m high [G]. Inside: turf fields, 8 wood courts, 4 indoor sand courts, batting cages,
two-storey fun centre (32 bowling lanes, arcade, laser tag), sports bar. Outside: four fenced 128 m turf squares
to the west, two ball diamonds, four soccer pitches, five sand courts, all lit.

**Buffalo Bill Museum** (199 N Front St, Le Claire; 41.5985, -90.3426). Long low building ~21 x 82 m along
the river (N–S): 1971 block, 2001 45 ft square addition, 2008 River Pilots annex enclosing the boat. Entrance
to Front St (west) [G]. **Lone Star** (1868 wooden sternwheel towboat, NHL): hull 27.4 x 7.5 m, ~32 m with the
wheel, two decks, pilot house on top; white #F2F0E8, red wheel #9E2A2B, black stacks. Exhibits: river pilots,
Sauk and Fox, pioneers, Buffalo Bill. **Riverboat Twilight** (1987 Victorian replica, 47.5 m, three decks)
docks alongside (pier 41.5994, -90.3423).

**Antique Archaeology** (115 Davenport St, off Cody Rd; 41.5957, -90.3445). Two-storey former fabrication shop
~9 x 15 m, plus a 1940s-garage-style annex on the lot; old cars out front (a Nash), rusty signs on the walls;
inside: show merchandise, motorcycles, signs.

**Le Claire / Cody Road** (US 67, 41.6028, -90.3439). Narrow two-storey Italianate brick fronts (#9C4A35),
parapets, metal cornices, limestone footings; land drops east to the river. Bard Hotel (3 storeys, 207 N Cody).
Green Tree marker (giant elm, died 1964) west of the museum by the picnic pavilion.

## River crossings / Arsenal

**I-74 bridge (2021)**: twin basket-handle true arches, one per carriageway (4 ribs), ribs lean in ~13°,
braced only by 2 crown struts + 2 intermediate struts. Main span 795 ft (242 m), arch top 245 ft above the river
(~50 m above the deck at ~18 m). Decks 72 ft (22 m) each, centrelines ~38 m apart, bearing ~349°. White
fluoropolymer paint [G, from photos]. Strand hangers. Approach girders weathering steel on Y piers. Night: LED
floodlights wash the arches from the floor beams; default scenes white piers + blue arches, Christmas red/green.
Bike path (14 ft) on the downstream (west) side with a midspan overlook. Arch centre ~41.5195, -90.5121.

**Clock Tower Building** (Storehouse A, 1864–67; tower 41.5167, -90.5639): 180 x 60 ft LeClaire limestone
(buff-grey #CFC4AE), three storeys + attic, gabled ends, central 25 ft square tower 117 ft tall on the NW face,
four 12 ft clock faces, pyramid roof. Interior: Corps of Engineers offices. Mississippi River Visitor Center
beside Lock 15 (41.5177, -90.5642).

**Stone Shops**: ten 1867–93 Joliet limestone shops in two rows along Rodman Ave (lat 41.5187 and 41.5170,
lon -90.5459…-90.5423); two-storey, arched windows, slate roofs [G]. Building 60 = Arsenal Museum.
**Quarters One** (41.5224, -90.5493): 1872 Italianate stone villa, 3 floors + tower.

**Lock and Dam 15**: 1,203 ft roller dam, 11 rollers between concrete piers, open-truss service bridge over the
top, set at an angle to the river (Iowa end 41.5197, -90.5706 -> Illinois end 41.5176, -90.5671). Main lock
110 x 600 ft (41.5177, -90.5650 -> 41.5190, -90.5633), auxiliary 110 x 360 ft alongside to the NW.
Government Bridge swing span on the lock wall.

## Moline / Rock Island

**John Deere Pavilion** (1400 River Dr; 41.5075, -90.5180): 14,000 sq ft glass and steel hall ~49 x 33 m,
grid rotated ~26°; green #367C2B steel, yellow #FFDE00 accents; old and new machines inside (4010 cutaway,
Model D, combine), glowing at night.

**John Deere World HQ** (Saarinen 1964; 41.4766, -90.4259): 7-storey Cor-Ten (#6A3B22) frame with louvred
sunshades over bronze glass, 100.6 x 29.3 m across a ravine; 2-storey display building 64 x 27 m to the east
(Girard mural inside); lakes with fountains to the south.

**Vibrant Arena at The MARK** (1201 River Dr; 41.5080, -90.5204): ~120 x 128 m, ~25–30 m [G], 12,000 seats
(Ellerbe Becket 1993); glass entry on River Dr; 61 ft LED "Vibrant Arena" roof sign with pinwheel logo;
purple #5B2C83 and charcoal #3A3A3C. Inside: 11,000 sq ft lobby, ring concourse, bowl.

**The District, Rock Island**: Fort Armstrong Theatre / Circa '21 (1828 3rd Ave, 41.5097, -90.5736): 1921
Art Deco, Indian-red brick #8B3A2B with ivory polychrome terra cotta, curved corner, chained marquee.
RIBCO (1815 2nd Ave): late-1800s brick. 2–4 storey brick blocks with cast-iron fronts.

**Augustana Old Main** (41.5044, -90.5496): 54 x 24 m, 3 storeys, buff LeClaire limestone #D8C9A3, central
pediment on four pilasters, copper dome with blue-green patina #6FA08F, balustrade and cupola.

## Airport / planes / others

**Quad Cities Intl (MLI)** (ARP 41.4482, -90.5078, 180 m): runways 9/27 3,049 m (091°), 13/31 2,225 m,
5/23 1,071 m, all 46 m wide. Terminal ~41.4535, -90.5064, gates on the south (apron) side, concourses A and B,
10 jet bridges; 2025 entry canopy, glass, terrazzo. Tower 41.4425, -90.5092 (white concrete, ~35 m [G]).
Elliott Aviation hangars south side (~132 x 71 m at 41.4423, -90.5068).

**Cessna 172**: 8.28 m long, 11.0 m span, 2.72 m high, strut-braced high wing, fixed tricycle gear, white with
a blue cheatline. **Air Tractor AT-502B**: 10.2 m long, 15.85 m span, 3.0 m high, low wing, taildragger, long
turboprop nose, cockpit well aft of the hopper, chrome yellow.

**Niabi Zoo** (Coal Valley; Discovery Center 2011 at 41.4439, -90.4342). **Watch Tower Lodge** (Black Hawk
SHS, 41.4643, -90.5728): 1930s CCC LeClaire limestone and hewn oak, three buildings joined by a walkway,
~86 x 32 m. **Geneseo City Park** (41.4487, -90.1566): gazebo, war memorials, storefronts on State and Main.
Nearest trap range: Bi-State Sportsman's Club, Colona (41.4896, -90.3418).

## Davenport interiors

- **Hotel Blackhawk**: two-storey lobby atrium under the restored skylight; marble floor (pink/green centre
  #D9A3A0 / #6E8B6A, black-white border); original 1915 front desk; mezzanine around; arched windows; Bix
  lounge bar; cream plaster #EDE3CF with dark wood #4A2E1F.
- **Adler Theatre**: two-storey Deco lobby (~22 ft) with a grand mahogany stair to the mezzanine, two crystal
  chandeliers, stepped coffered ceiling; Dreyfuss auditorium, gold/ivory (#C9A24A/#EFE6D2), burgundy seats
  #6B1E2A, one balcony, ~50 ft proscenium.
- **Figge**: terrazzo grand lobby (#CFCBC4), white walls, fritted glass, wide stair to upper white galleries
  with pale oak floors.
- **Davenport Bank & Trust**: three-storey banking hall, painted groin vaults (cream/red/blue/gold), tall
  arched windows on Main St, wrought-iron teller cages, walnut, marble floor, Black Hawk treaty mural (south).
- **City Hall**: 1980 interior, council chambers on the 1st floor: raised curved oak dais, carpet, flags.
- **RiverCenter**: glass atrium concourse, Great River Hall (32,400 sq ft) with exposed trusses, concrete floor.
- **Ambrose Hall**: central stair hall behind the tower door, double-loaded corridors, oak trim, cream plaster.
- **Vander Veer conservatory** (1955): glass house with a raised centre, beds of seasonal flowers, paths, pool.
- **NorthPark**: single-level branching mall, ~40 ft concourses, skylights, beige tile, centre court.
