## The town
- Henry County city on the Green River, in the Quad Cities metro. Pop. 5,045 (2020). ZIP 61241. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colona,_Illinois))
- Created in 1997 by merging the City of Green Rock and the Village of Colona. It was the first Illinois merger by popular vote (approved 1995). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colona,_Illinois))
- It sits in the Rock River valley, where the Green River and the Hennepin Canal flow into the Rock. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colona,_Illinois))
- On the map, Green Rock is the numbered grid west of the Green River (1st–8th Streets, 1st–11th Avenues). Old Colona is the small grid east of the river and the IAIS railroad: Maple, Warren, Water, Broadway, Green River and Elm Streets, plus Old Colona Park and Lock 28. This split is inferred from OSM street and park names and is not confirmed by a source.
- Schools: Colona School District 190 runs one school, Colona Grade School, at 700 1st Street. High school students go to United Township HSD 30. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colona,_Illinois), [SchoolDigger](https://www.schooldigger.com/go/IL/district/10690/search.aspx))
- Transit: MetroLINK Route 30. The OSM stops include the shelters at 1 St & 9 Ave and 6 St & 7 Ave. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colona,_Illinois), OSM)

## Bridges (FHWA NBI via BridgeLookup)
- **IL 84 over the Rock River**, NBI 037012800000000. Built 1981. Steel continuous box-beam spans, 6 main spans (max 53.6 m), 299.9 m long, deck 28.4 m wide, 4 lanes. 12,400 vehicles a day. ([BridgeLookup](https://bridgelookup.com/bridge/123394))
- **IL 84 over the Green River**, "S Edge Colona", NBI 037008000000000. Built 1936, rebuilt 1993. Steel continuous stringer / multi-beam, 4 main spans (max 21.9 m), 76.8 m long, deck 13.2 m wide, 2 lanes. ([BridgeLookup](https://bridgelookup.com/bridge/123372))
- **I-80** crosses the Rock River just north of these bounds, and the Green River and the IAIS railroad near Exit 7. IDOT is replacing the I-80 decks over the Rock River and Cleveland Road. ([Yahoo/WQAD](https://www.yahoo.com/news/interstate-80-begins-henry-county-234024096.html))

## Hennepin Canal
- Concrete locks, 170 x 35 ft (52 x 10.7 m). The Hennepin was the first American canal built of concrete without cut-stone facings. It opened in 1907. Most gates were later replaced by concrete walls, so water spills over them in small falls. A few locks had unique Marshall gates. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hennepin_Canal), [IDNR](https://dnr.illinois.gov/parks/about/park.hennepincanal.html))
- The canal joins the Rock River at **Lock 29**. The City of Colona has a park and boat launch there, and the Green River enters the Rock about a quarter mile away. The IDNR trail is surfaced from Bureau Junction to Colona. ([Rock River Trail](https://rockrivertrail.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/RRWT_Description_K.pdf), [IDNR](https://dnr.illinois.gov/recreation/greenwaysandtrails/hennepincanal.html))
- OSM: Lock 29 is way 1052166659 (15666, 5819 to 15602, 5812), with a weir at its upstream end and the "Rock River-Hennepin Canal" slipway at (15565, 5784). Lock 28 is way 1052166663 (19134, 4653 to 19090, 4707) in old Colona.
